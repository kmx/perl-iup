[% h.name %]

[% n.iupglseparator %]

[% h.desc %]

A separator line drawn by IUP between GL controls in an
L<IUP::GL::CanvasBox|IUP::GL::CanvasBox>. It displays a vertical or
horizontal line and exists only inside an
L<IUP::GL::CanvasBox|IUP::GL::CanvasBox>.

[% h.usage %]

[% h.create %]

 my $sep = IUP::GL::Separator->new();

B<Returns:> the identifier of the created element, or C<undef> if an error occurs.

[% h.notes %]

The L<IUP::GL::Separator|IUP::GL::Separator> visual is controlled by the
L<IUP::GL::SubCanvas|IUP::GL::SubCanvas> attributes BORDERWIDTH and
BORDERCOLOR.

The natural size will be BORDERWIDTH in one direction and it will expand
if there is free space in the other direction.
